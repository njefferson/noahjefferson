## What the figures cannot show

- The pieces differ in size, so no figure here is a cost for the same work done two ways.
- No dollars are known for one plan, because the instrument derives none and the harness's running figure covers the whole session.
- Only the third act ran wholly the new way, and it is one piece.
- The instrument counts a plan's part from its approval to the next approval, so turns spent scoping the next piece fall in the earlier plan's part.
