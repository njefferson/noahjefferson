webapp:// test

Four taps. Each one says what its result means. Nothing here writes anything except test 2 and 3, and both label what they wrote.

1  Does webapp:// open the installed app?

Quietkeep opens → the scheme works and the destination problem is solvable.
Safari opens, or nothing happens → it does not work here, and that is the end of the idea.

2  Does it carry the text?

App opens AND says “Held from a link” → the path and query survive; the research was wrong and this route works today.
App opens with nothing held → the query was dropped, as reported. The app is right, the note is gone.

3  Same again, other edition

Only meaningful if you have the non-Sync Quietkeep on your Home Screen too. Skip it if you do not.

4  The control — an ordinary link

Expected to open Safari and hold the text there. If this opens the installed app instead, then everything above is measuring the wrong thing and iOS is capturing links after all.

Anything held by 2, 3 or 4 is labelled WEBAPPTEST-… so you can find and bin it. Test 4 in particular lands in Safari’s copy, which is the whole point of the exercise.
