Which worker is your iPad actually holding?

Both taps failed the same way — which may mean the fix does not work, or may mean it never ran. A new service worker WAITS until you press the update control; it does not take over on its own. So if you have opened staging before, the worker holding that address is an old one, and the fix has never executed on your device. The error naming a service worker is itself proof one is in charge.

1.40.1 or lower → the fix never ran. Look for the strip saying a newer version is ready, press it, then come back and tap A again.
