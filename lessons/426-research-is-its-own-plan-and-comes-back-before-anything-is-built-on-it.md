## 426 · Research is its own plan and comes back before anything is built on it

**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST research-returns-first — a question that needs reading is answered by a plan that does the reading alone, and the build plan is written from the answer, never inside the same run as the reading.

A plan that read the photographs' Lightroom edits and then built a colour route on that reading in the same run would have shown the reading only at its end, eight hours in, with every decision between made by the session. Cut to the reading alone, the same work took three hours, and the decision about what to build stayed where it belongs.

The smallest thing that answers the question comes first, then the answer, then a build plan written from the answer. Always, and not only when the question looks large.
