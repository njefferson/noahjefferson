## 428 · Counsel offered against the system's own reasons

**Enforced by:** JUDGEMENT — whether a proposal to read less, print less or summarise more has taken apart a rule whose reason is written beside it is a reading of the proposal's own reasons, and no script can make that reading.

**Smell:** a proposal to read less of a report, to print less of a status, or to load less of the rules, offered as an improvement, with the rule it would weaken left standing and its written reason never answered.

Asked to think about what was missing, the session proposed four changes and withdrew each a turn later. It proposed a status that prints only what changed. It proposed a doctrine read of changed lines after a resume. It proposed that agents start from a summary section of a report. Each was withdrawn for its reason. A status that prints less hides a red walk, as a measurement from early October showed. The whole doctrine is read after a compaction because the context is the only memory, and a compaction replaces it with a paraphrase that drifts. A report's caveats hide in its prose, so the remedy is to say what a report must contain, not to read less of it.

The other rules were restated with their reasons, so that they decide the next time. A rule is a gate because routing around it looks like judgement from inside. Research is separate from building because a stacked plan buries the decision. There is no budget because a budget becomes the target. A correction holds writes because a session once worked through nine corrections on its own reading. A refusal latches because a second route is the same bypass. These reasons are written down so that they decide the next time, and the mood of the turn does not.
