## 385 · The apparatus built to hold the main thread was loaded into the context it holds, so each addition brought the next compaction nearer

**Enforced by:** GATE noahjefferson:doctrine-read-guard.mjs — the doctrine due after a compaction or a resume is the lines from the heading of §0 up to the heading of §3, every line at a startup, and the line printed at SessionStart and the refusal both name the lines due · GATE noahjefferson:compact-recall.mjs — after a compaction it prints only the owner's messages typed after the newest assistant reply, the newest ten at most, then the count of the rest and the path of a file holding all of them · GATE noahjefferson:session-brief.mjs — after a compaction or a resume, or where it cannot read the due marker, it prints the lessons as a count and the folder's path, with the reason on its own line, and every title only at a startup · GATE noahjefferson:session-guards.test.mjs — cases for each of the three, each with plants that take one check out and must fail it · JUDGEMENT — before adding a rule, a gate or a print that reaches a session by being loaded or printed into it, work out what it adds to every reload of that session's context, and what the session would lose if it were printed as a pointer to a file

**Smell:** a gate whose output is printed at every SessionStart or every owner message; a rule written into a file every session loads, to hold a session that has already forgotten the rule it replaces; a compaction that comes sooner each time the apparatus around it grows.

**Recorded 2026-10-05.** The apparatus that holds the main thread to the owner's rules is made of text, and a session meets text by having it loaded or printed into its context. Three of those prints came back together after every compaction, at the SessionStart the harness fires then, before any work: the whole doctrine had to be read (§383), every owner message since the previous compaction was printed back verbatim (§376), and the session brief printed every lesson's title. Each was added to make the next failure impossible, and each was sound taken alone. Together they were put into the very context that a compaction exists to relieve.

**Measured on 2026-10-05**, by counting lines in the working copy. `DOCTRINE.md` was 2494 lines and the lessons index 392 titles, all of which came back after every compaction, and the owner's messages came back without a limit: every message since the previous compaction, which in a long run is most of what the session had been told. A compaction fires on the size of the context, so the next one comes when the context fills again. What a compaction frees is spent at once on reloading the apparatus, and every rule added to the loaded set makes the reload larger and the interval to the next compaction shorter. After each compaction the main thread then had to choose its next move from what remained, and the apparatus for stopping it choosing from memory was the thing that had filled the room.

**The fix bounds what a compaction or a resume brings back, and keeps what a startup brings.**

- **The doctrine.** At a startup every line is still due. After a compaction or a resume the lines due are those from the heading of §0 up to the heading of §3, lines 15 to 654 when this was written, 640 of 2494, about a quarter of the file. They hold the rules the main thread acts on. The marker records the SessionStart's source and the gate reads the range from the file's headings each time, so it cannot go stale as the file grows. A doctrine whose headings cannot be found is read in full, because a gate that cannot find its range must not read less.
- **The owner's messages.** After a compaction only those typed after the newest assistant reply before it come back, since a reply answers everything before it. The newest ten are printed, oldest first, because a later message overrides an earlier one, then the count of the rest and the path of a file holding all of them, verbatim. A message missing from the print is one Read away.
- **The lessons.** The brief prints their count and the folder's path after a compaction or a resume, and every title at a startup. It finds out which start this is from the due marker, by the session id on its standard input, and where it cannot (the marker is not written yet, was removed once the doctrine had been read, or no payload reached it) it prints the short form and says why on its own line, because the short form is the one that costs nothing to be wrong about.

**What the bound cannot see.** A resume is treated as a compaction on the reasoning that the resumed conversation already carried what was read; nothing checks that it did. The doctrine's later sections, such as §11b on credit and §14 on debugging, are no longer demanded after a compaction, and are read when work reaches them; the hub's CLAUDE.md index, loaded every turn, still carries each of them in a line. The brief runs from `.claude/hooks/session-start.sh`, wired in `.claude/settings.json` and also run by `hook-dispatch.mjs` through each repo it touches. The dispatcher writes the marker before it runs a repo's hooks, so on that route the brief can read it. The project's own SessionStart entry runs beside the dispatcher with no order between them, so on that route a startup may print the short form with its reason.

**The general form.** A control that works by being put in front of a session is paid for out of the same context it is trying to protect, and the payment recurs at every reload. Count that payment when the control is added, and prefer the form that points at a file the session opens when the work needs it. Three controls that were each worth their cost became, together, the largest thing the compaction brought back.
