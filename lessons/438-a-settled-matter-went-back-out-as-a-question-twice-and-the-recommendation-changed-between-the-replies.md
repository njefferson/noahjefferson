## 438 · A settled matter went back out as a question twice and the recommendation changed between the replies, because the record of the day it was settled and the rules file's header were read after the question and not before

**Enforced by:** JUDGEMENT — before a closing question goes out, the record of the day the matter was settled is read for its answer: the report of the step that did the work, the manager's notes of that day, the plan's Asked section and the header of the file the matter concerns; a question the record answers is dropped and not asked, and a recommendation is changed between two replies only on a new fact that the reply names

**Smell:** a closing question about what was done, which file, which route or which rule, whose answer sits in an earlier step's report or in the notes of the day before; the same matter asked a second time; a recommendation that differs from the one in the previous reply with no new fact named; and the tell, that the record was read after the question went out and not before it.

**Recorded 2026-10-29.** Measured on 28 October, twice. A question about which file an edit had been made on went to the person deciding, though step 212's report and the manager's notes of 27 October already named it; neither had been read before the question went. The question was dropped as work handed back once the record was read, and the notes now say so (Found 0.18). A matter that a standing order had already settled, how a change to the agent rules file is landed, went out as a choice twice, and the recommendation changed between the two replies; the header of the rules file, which carries the rule the order settles, was read only after the first reply (Found 220.10, with the decision recorded beside Found 220.3). Each question cost a message and a turn, and the second of the two cost a reply that contradicted the first.

**Beside lesson 258.** Lesson 258 says a defect with a known fix is not a decision and that a well-formed closing list can be made entirely of things that are not choices. This is the same fault with the answer known in place of the fix: a matter the record has answered is not a decision either. Both were obeyed to the letter in form. The list was short, numbered, with a recommendation first, and its contents were not choices.

**The test.** Before a question goes out, answer two things. Does a report, a note or a header already say which way this goes? And is the recommendation the one the last reply gave, and if not, what is the new fact? A question costs the person deciding a message; a read of the record costs a call, and the read comes first.

**What cannot be gated.** Whether the record answers a question is a reading of the record, and no parser makes it. The stop guard can see the shape of a closing list and cannot see whether the list's contents were already settled elsewhere. What can be done is to leave the settled thing where the next reading finds it: the decision line in the manager's notes carries the day and the reason, and the file the matter concerns says the rule in its own header, as the rules file now does.

**The general form.** A question put to the person deciding is a spend. The record of the day a matter was settled is the cheaper reader, and it is opened before the question, since a recommendation that changes between two replies shows that the record was not.
