## 350 · If you can write the recommendation yourself, it was never a decision — and the closing list is where settled work gets laundered back

**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST recommendation-test — before any question reaches the
owner, write the recommendation first. If it can be written with confidence from
a rule, a record or a measurement already in the repository, the question is not
a question: delete it and do the work. The recommendation is not a courtesy
attached to a choice; it is the test of whether a choice exists.
**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST filter-not-quota — "a report ends in decisions" is a
FILTER, not a slot to fill. When nothing is genuinely the owner's, the honest
close is that nothing is waiting on them. A closing list padded to three is the
failure the rule was written to prevent, wearing the rule's own clothes.
**Enforced by:** JUDGEMENT — no parser can tell a real decision from a settled
one, because both arrive as a sentence with options in it. The only instrument
is the recommendation, and it is an instrument the session has to aim at itself.

**Smell:** a closing list of numbered questions where each recommendation cites a rule, a record or a measurement already in the repository, and none is hedged. Also: a reply that quotes the governing rule and then asks whether it applies.

**Three times in one session, settled matters were handed back as numbered
decisions with recommendations attached.** Each one was already answered, in
writing, in a file the session had open — and in two of the three the session
had QUOTED the governing rule in the same reply that then asked about it.

- Whether a documentation change could merge without the on-device gate. The
  release flow names the file and the exemption in one sentence.
- Whether to fix a reader-facing defect whose remedy was known and whose cost
  was one commit. The doctrine says a defect you know how to fix is not a
  decision, and says it in those words.
- Whether to stamp the version after a capability release. The versioning rule
  says the middle number bumps with EVERY capability release; the only
  versioning call that is ever the owner's is an identity change.

## The tell, and it is exact

**Every laundered item carried a recommendation, and the recommendation was
correct.** That is the whole diagnostic. A real decision is one where the record
cannot rank the options — so if the ranking can be written down confidently,
the ranking has already happened and what is being sent is not a question but a
delay.

The doctrine already required the recommendation to come first. That instruction
was followed to the letter and its purpose was inverted: it exists to make a hard
choice cheap to answer, and it was used to make settled work look deliberative.

## Why it happens after good work, not before

**Momentum is the condition, not ignorance.** All three arrived at the end of
long, correct stretches — after the verification passed, after the gates went
green. At that moment the closing list feels like diligence and stopping feels
responsible, while "here is what I am doing next" feels presumptuous.

That instinct is backwards. Handing back determined work is not deference; it
costs a round trip, it arrives in the middle of something else, and it teaches
that the rule was optional — which is the opposite of what writing it down was
for. Deference that returns settled work is a bill, not a courtesy.

## What it took to stop

**Two corrections from outside the session**, and the second had to name the
pattern rather than the instance — the first correction fixed one item and the
next reply produced another list. A session cannot see this from the inside for
the same reason it cannot judge its own scope: what is left after the rules stop
being re-read is a paraphrase, and a paraphrase of "end in decisions" is "end
with a decisions section".

**The general shape: a rule that names a SECTION will be satisfied by producing
that section.** Anything in a report whose shape is fixed in advance — a
decisions list, a risks list, a next-steps list — will be filled to its expected
length by whatever is nearest, and what is nearest at the end of a long run is
the work just finished. The remedy is to make the rule name a TEST rather than a
shape, which is what the recommendation-test above is.
