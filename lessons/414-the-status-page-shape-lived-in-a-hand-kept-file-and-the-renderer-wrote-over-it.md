## 414 · The status page's shape lived in a hand-kept file that the renderer wrote over at every status, so the renderer now draws it: versions, a chip for each step in colour and in words, what is open for the owner, and what is found and not fixed

**Enforced by:** GATE hub:session-guards.test.mjs — the case that drives `statusPage` from a planted notes file and a planted state and reads back each step's chip, what each section carries and what the page leaves out, with plants in `report.mjs` for every part that must fail it

**Recorded 2026-10-19.** The page was rewritten whole at every status and at every agent's hand-back, from the pointer and the manager's notes. What it drew was the record: an outcome, the explanations standing and ruled out, the decisions, and three groups of Found lines. The page the owner read as a picture of the work, with a heading, what production and staging carry, one block for each step with a state, what is open and what was found and not fixed, was a file kept by hand beside it, and the next rewrite replaced it. A shape that lives only in a file the renderer writes over is not kept.

**What is done now.** `statusPage` in `report.mjs` draws the page in that shape. A heading and an updated line with the last status's time in California time; `Version:` lines of `status/manager.txt` under Versions; one section for each step in Order and each Standing step, its heading the step's number and its title from the plan, with a chip in colour and in words (`stepChip`): green `done` for a hand-back opening DONE or a `Done N:` line, since the main thread counts a step done by decision whatever its newest end; amber `running`; amber `under review` for the step the unnumbered `Next:` line names first while a review runs; red `open` for a refusal, a failure or an interruption with no DONE; purple `held` with what it waits on. Under the heading, what it is (the `What N:` line, else the title up to its first comma), where it stands (the first line of the newest hand-back; `ended by an interruption, with no hand-back` where the newest end was not a report, because the last text of such an end is a tool result; `running, no hand-back yet`; or `not sent`), a `Counted done:` line, and the `Why N:` and `Next N:` lines. The Standing review step is one `Reviews` line with the newest verdict's first words and no chip (`reviewWords`). Then Open for you, from the `Open:` lines, and Found and not fixed: of the Found lines of the plan's own steps, those sorted as stopping the plan or queued, tagged in words, and a count of those not sorted yet. The explanations, the decisions, the outcome and the fixed Found lines stay in the notes and are not drawn. `managerNotes` reads the six new keys, and a numbered `Next 41:` and the unnumbered `Next:` do not read each other's line. The colours are six-digit tokens with a dark pair, there is no table, and every bullet is on a line of its own.

**What the check cannot see.** It checks the structure and the words. It cannot see whether the page reads well on a phone, and it cannot make the manager write the lines: a step with no `What` line shows its title up to the first comma, and a page with no `Open:` line falls back on what the plan itself leaves open. The page draws a Found line that stops the plan beside the queued ones, because a finding that stops the plan must not vanish from the page that is read; and it draws only the Found lines of the plan's own steps, because a session's older reports hold hundreds of lines from earlier plans, which the notes queue already carries (a render from a session's real state showed 196 of them on one page before the filter).

**The general form.** A shape the owner reads belongs in the renderer that rewrites it, with a plant for every part, because a shape kept in a file by hand goes at the next write.
