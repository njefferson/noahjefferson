## 401 · A status repeated the goals, every hand-back and every Found line each time, so the print outgrew what the conversation would carry, and now it shows what changed and nothing twice

**Enforced by:** GATE hub:session-guards.test.mjs — the case for a status that shows what changed and nothing twice (the goals once per session, a hand-back once, a Found line while it is new, changed or unsorted, a running agent's hook refusal once, the approval state in full only when it changed, the main thread's reads since the last status, no hand-back text a second time under the ended agents, and every line again when there is no session id), with plants that take one of those out and must fail it

**Recorded 2026-10-18.** A status printed the standing goals, the newest hand-back of every step with all its lines, every Found line of every report file, every hook refusal of every running agent and the whole approval state, at every status. A plan with many steps and many findings grew a print past what one tool result carries, and the harness cut it to a preview and saved the rest, which is how the manager came to read about 2 KB of a print of about 120 KB. The print was long because it repeated, not because it knew more.

**What it prints now.** `report.mjs` keeps a record of what the session's statuses printed (`~/.claude/report-seen/<session>.json`, `readSeen` and `writeSeen`) and prints the difference. The pointer line, the Order states and the Review line print every time: they are one line each and they are what changed. The goals print at the first status of a session and at no other; every owner message prints them again anyway. A hand-back prints once, under its step, and the ended-agents list says "printed above under step N" in place of its text. A Found line prints when it is new, when its text or its sort or the manager's note changed, and while it is unsorted; a sorted line an earlier status printed is counted and left to the page. A running agent's hook refusal prints the status that first meets it, with the number of new ones. The approval state prints in full only when it differs from the last, else as one line saying it is unchanged and whether the plan is in force. The status page, rewritten whole, keeps the entire record.

**What it fails towards.** A status with no session id, or a record that cannot be read or written, prints everything, as it always did. The record is written after the print and holds the marks of what was printed, so a failure to write costs a repeat in the next status, never a line that was never shown.

**What the check cannot see.** Whether a line the manager did not need to see twice was worth seeing once. And a context that was compacted loses what the earlier statuses printed while the record still says they were printed: the page, which the first read after a compaction takes in whole, is what carries it across.

**The general form.** A report that repeats what the reader already holds grows until the tool that carries it cuts it, and the reader then reads the cut. A report is the difference since the last one, and the whole is kept where it can be read whole.
