## 417 · The judgement before a send was one free sentence and the step about to go had none, so the gate wants five named parts for the hand-back and four for the step, in a plan with an Order or without one

**Enforced by:** GATE hub:session-guards.test.mjs — the cases for the judgement gate and the judgement state (a send is refused until status/manager.txt holds, written after the newest hand-back of a step of the plan, a line "Judged N:" with the parts is, why, route, fails and cheapest for each end of that step, and a line "Why M:" with the parts now, wrong, fails and cheapest for each earlier end of the step being sent and one more; a line short of a part or with a part under ten characters judges nothing; a Standing step goes without; a plan with no Order is gated by its Steps; the refusal names the line to write and does not latch) and for the Judgement line every status prints, with plants that take out the part test, the length test, the count of Why lines, the Why requirement and the gating of a plan with no Order, and each plant must fail its case

**Recorded 2026-10-20.** Lesson 407 made the manager write one line for each hand-back before the next step went, and the gate counted lines. A line could be any sentence, so it forced a judgement to exist and nothing about what the judgement covered. The thinking before a send has parts that were never named: what the hand-back showed, why it ended as it did, what that means for the route, where the route fails next, and the cheapest route from here that holds the owner's rules; and, for the step about to go, why now, what would make it wrong, where it fails next, and why it is the cheapest route. The gate also read only a plan with an Order, so a plan without one, such as the plan these lines were written under, could send every step with no judgement at all.

**What is done now.** `missingParts` in `report.mjs` reads a line after its key, splits it on a space, a bar and a space, and names each required label that is missing or carries fewer than ten characters (`PART_MIN`); a segment that opens with no label belongs to the part before it. `managerNotes` keeps every `Judged N:` line and every `Why N:` line in file order (`whys`). `stepNumbers` gives a plan's Order where it has one and its `## Steps` where it has not. `judgedState` takes the step being sent: it counts the whole `Judged` lines of the step of the newest end against that step's ends, requires the notes file to have been written after that end, and then counts the whole `Why` lines of the step being sent against its earlier ends plus one. Its kinds are the old ones and `incomplete`, `why-none`, `why-short` and `why-incomplete`. `judgedGate` in `hook-dispatch.mjs` refuses on any of them, names the file and the whole line to write, fails closed when the agents cannot be read, passes a Standing step, and does not latch. The status prints the Judgement line from the same function, and `DOCTRINE.md` rule 2 of §0e says so in one sentence.

**What the check cannot see.** Whether any part is true or says anything: ten characters of filler satisfy it. It forces each part to be written and fresh; the reading of the hand-back stays the manager's, and the owner's reading of the page is what weighs it. A `Why` line written for one step and left in the file counts for no other step, but a line left from an earlier send of the same step counts when the file has been written to since for another reason, which the count of lines against sends only narrows.

**The general form.** A record that something was thought is only as good as the form it must take. Name the parts the thinking has to cover, and make the send depend on every part being there.
