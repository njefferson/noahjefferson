## 472 · A plan typed from memory of the record reached its review with facts the record already held, and each miss cost a review and two of them a button

**Enforced by:** GATE hub:plan-seed.mjs — one command prints, at the write, the item's own entry from the program with its own dependency line, the goals block, the rules version, the Files list in the shape the plan guard reads, the close step with its three acts, the review step, the open found keys and the remote's heads marked as movable; the print is held by cases and plants in session-guards.test.mjs

**Recorded 2026-11-05.** Measured on 5 November, California time, in two plans in two sessions.

**What happened.** Both plans were written from the writer's memory of the record while every fact they needed sat in a file. One took a dependency line from the entry above the item's own, copied a Files list from an older plan's shape, and left the filing out of its close step. The other carried a remote head as a fact after another session had moved it, and wrote a proof as zero findings without reading how the tool counts. Each miss was found by a review agent after the plan was typed, and two of them sent the plan back through plan mode for a second press of the button.

**What was wrong.** Every check read the plan after it was typed. Nothing put the record into the plan at the moment of writing, so the writer's memory of a page stood in for the page. The session brief had already fixed the same cause at the start of a session with a print; the plan's moment had been left as reading.

**The test.** A plan starts as a print of the record read from the files at that moment, the writer adds only the item's steps, and a fact another session can move is marked so that the step reads it at the time of use.

**The general form.** A fact needed at a write belongs in front of the writer in the write's own shape, because a refusal after the write costs a round trip and a print before it costs none.
