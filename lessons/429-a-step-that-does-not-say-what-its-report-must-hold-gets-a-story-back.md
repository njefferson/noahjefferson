## 429 · A step that does not say what its report must hold gets a story back

**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST report-contents-named — every step names the contents of its report, in order: the figures by name, the files by path, each check with its exit code, what was asked and not done with why, every host and wall, every finding as a line opening Found, and every doubt as a line opening Uncertain.

Reports of fifty lines were read whole to find six figures, and the caveats sat inside the prose, where a reader passing over the paragraph would miss them. Nothing in the step had said what the report was for, so the report was written as a story of the run.

Every step now names its report's contents in order, and the agent writes exactly those. The figures are named, the files are given by their full paths, each check is listed with its exit code, and what was asked but not done is listed with the reason. A caveat then reaches the reader as a caveat, on a line of its own, and the story of the run belongs in the progress note. The hub plan adds the same line to the agent rules.
