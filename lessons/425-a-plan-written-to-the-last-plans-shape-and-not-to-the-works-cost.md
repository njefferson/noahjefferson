## 425 · A plan written to the last plan's shape and not to the work's cost

**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST cost-by-what-changed — every check, walk and render a plan names is costed from its steps before the plan goes up, and each one runs once, on what changed, in the last step, never once per round of a sweep.

Measured on 28 October: an integration step ran all fifty-three walks across five rounds. One round was cut by the shell's two-hour limit. The walks ran twenty-five checks on each of eleven states, made thirty-six renders, and the step took five hours and eight minutes. The plan written next copied that shape, with eight reviews, five worktrees and thirty renders, and it was stopped before its first step at an estimate of ten to fifteen agent hours.

The plan had been written to the shape of the plan before it, not to the work in front of it. Nobody set what each check cost against what had changed, so the cost of the last run was carried forward as if it were the method.

The method is that a plan is written by its cost. Every check, walk and render runs once, on what changed, in the last step. The gate and the review that would refuse the shapes above are still to be landed in the hub plan; until they are, the cost is read off the steps by the author before the plan is shown.
