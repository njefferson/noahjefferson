## 387 · The harness fires resume events for a session nobody left, and the doctrine gate re-arms on each, so a turn after one spends two reads before its first call

**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST resume-reads-first — when the top of a turn carries a resume line, the doctrine reads come first, before any other call: the lines the line printed at SessionStart names, read from the live clone's copy at /root/.claude/hub/DOCTRINE.md, whatever the turn is about

**Recorded 2026-10-07.** The harness fires a resume for a session the person using it has not left. Each one arrives as a SessionStart whose source is `resume`, and `doctrine-read-guard.mjs` treats it as it treats any resume: it writes the due marker again, and the main thread may only read and give the status until Read results cover the lines due. On 2026-10-06 that happened four times. A turn that began after one spent two reads on the doctrine before it could make its first call of its own, and the first call the turn tried was refused until it had.

**Why the gate does not tell the two apart.** `BOUNDED_SOURCES` in `doctrine-read-guard.mjs` is the list of SessionStart sources after which only the lines from the heading of §0 up to the heading of §3 are due, and `resume` is on it (LESSONS §385). The event the harness sends carries the same source whether the resume was the harness's own or somebody's return to a session they had left, and nothing else in the payload separates them. A gate that tried would be guessing at a difference the event does not carry, and a gate that guessed wrong would let a session read less of the doctrine than it was told to, which is the failure §383 records. So the cost stays, bounded: two reads of about a quarter of the file, never the whole.

**What to do with it.** Treat a resume line at the top of a turn as the first instruction of that turn. Read the doctrine lines it names, from the live clone, before the call the turn was going to make, and then make that call. Doing it in that order costs the same two reads and no refusal; doing it the other way costs the same two reads and a refused call first.

**What the checklist cannot see.** Nothing counts the resume lines a session has had, so a turn that skips the reads is found by the refusal and not before it. The refusal is the gate working, and the reads it asks for are the ones in the checklist item above.
