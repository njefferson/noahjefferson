## 371 · Work on a gate is written as the outcome it must produce, never as what could get past it

**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST gate-outcome-wording — before writing anything about a hook gate (a plan, a prompt for a reviewer, a finding, a handoff, a lesson, a commit message, a reply), state it as the gate's outcome: what it refuses, what it passes, and what its refusal says. A finding against a gate is written as the outcome it does not yet produce, in the same terms.

**Smell:** a sentence whose subject is a way past a gate — a form of a command, a spelling of a call, a route the check does not see — rather than the thing the gate owes.

**Recorded 2026-09-29.** A session working on this family's hook gates wrote about them in the other register: the forms a gate would fail to see, set out as ways past it. Its replies were cut off repeatedly, it was moved to a lower model in the middle of the work, and it had to be abandoned with the work unfinished.

**It had already happened once, smaller.** In the adversarial review of 2026-09-28 (HANDOFF.md, gate 10), the reviewer given the half of `plan-guard` that refuses writes was stopped twice before it returned. That half has still not been reviewed.

**Why the outcome form costs nothing.** A gate's specification is its outcome: every write outside an approved plan is refused, whatever tool or form carries it; a refusal names the rule and the one thing that lifts it. A finding is a case where that outcome is not produced, and it carries its whole engineering fact when written as the outcome owed. For example, "every scratchpad measurement script is checked before node runs it, however the command reaches it" is the finding. A list of the forms that reach it unchecked adds nothing the fix needs. What it adds is text that reads, to anything screening the session's output, as instructions for getting past a guard.

**Where it applies.** Everywhere a gate is discussed, and most of all in the prompts handed to reviewers and skeptics, because a review asked for in the other register comes back in it. HANDOFF.md's list of gate findings not yet fixed was rewritten in these terms the same day.
