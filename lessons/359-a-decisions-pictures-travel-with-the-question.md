## 359 · A decision's pictures travel with the question, every time it is asked

**Enforced by:** CHECKLIST ask-with-the-pictures — a question whose answer is a
choice between pictures is sent with those pictures in the same message,
directly beside the question, every time the question is asked or re-asked.
Never "the pictures were sent earlier", never a pointer up the conversation,
and never a long report placed between the pictures and the question.

**Smell:** a closing list of decisions that says the comparison sheets "were
sent in the chat", or that re-sends them at the top of a reply and asks about
them at the bottom, under a screen or more of status.

**Measured 2026-09-24 in Jefferson-Photography-Studio.** Two layout decisions,
the editor's top bar (three candidates) and the colour-file browser (four),
were asked in a closing list across several replies. Their comparison sheets
had been rendered and sent hours earlier. On an iPad those sheets had scrolled
out of reach, so the questions could not be answered where they were asked.
They were then re-sent in the middle of a long reply, and a report of several
screens followed them before the questions came, which put them out of reach
again. Nobody can pick between pictures they cannot see.

**Why it keeps happening.** Sending the pictures once feels like having shown
them, and the rule that already exists, that a look choice is shown rather than
described, is satisfied by the first send. The rule is about the moment of
answering, not the moment of rendering. Chat scrolls, and on an iPad anything
more than a screen away is not in front of the reader.
