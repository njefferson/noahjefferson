## 431 · A decision the work cannot proceed without stops the work

**Enforced by:** JUDGEMENT — whether the next step depends on the answer is read from the plan's own steps and the question, and no script can tell a needed decision from one that could wait.

**Smell:** a build continued past a choice that the next step depends on, building on ground about to move; or a question put forward early whose answer no step depends on, which spends attention that the end of the plan would have used for it.

The test is whether the next step depends on the answer. A decision the work cannot proceed without stops the work, and the main thread comes to the person deciding then, with the options and the recommendation first. A decision the work can proceed without goes to the end of the plan. Working past a needed decision builds on ground about to move. Bringing forward a decision that nothing depends on spends that person's time on a question the end of the plan would have carried anyway.

Pictures go with a choice only where the choice is about how a photograph looks, because prose about colour is not an appearance. Every other choice is set out as options in words, answerable in one word.

On 28 October the rule was restated as pictures for every choice, with its reason left out. A rule applied without its reason is applied wrong, and this one was.
