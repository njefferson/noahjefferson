## 408 · A plan rested on sentences from a conversation and never accounted for the roadmap, so ExitPlanMode refuses a plan whose What is bullets quote no file or whose Roadmap leaves an item out

**Enforced by:** GATE hub:session-guards.test.mjs — the cases for the What is check (each bullet quotes twelve characters or more in double quotes, names a file, a repository the Files names holds that file, and every quoted stretch is in it, an ellipsis standing for words left out; a bullet that fails any of those is listed), for the Roadmap check (every open item of a repository's Next capability release has a bullet opening with its key, then in or out, then a reason; done items, sub-bullets and items outside the section are not asked for) and for the range line in a Found answered section, with plants that take out each check and each wiring into the plan guard

**Recorded 2026-10-19.** A plan stated what it rested on in its own sentences, and a sentence is the drifted paraphrase: nothing tied it to a line a reader could open. Separately, a plan named a repository and said nothing about the open items of that repository's roadmap, so the work it left out was left out by omission rather than by a reason.

**What the plan guard does now.** In `hook-dispatch.mjs`, `whatIsProblem` reads the plan's `## What is` bullets and refuses the plan when one quotes nothing, names no file outside the quote, names a file no repository the Files names holds, or quotes words the file does not carry. `roadmapProblem` reads each such repository's `- [ ] **Title**` items under `## Next capability release` (`roadmapItems`, the decision number from the marker else the title as key) and refuses the plan when an item has no bullet in `## Roadmap` that opens with the key, says `in` or `out`, and gives a reason of ten characters or more. `plan-guard.mjs` calls both at `ExitPlanMode`, after the findings check. A `## Found answered` line may answer a run of queued findings a plan leaves untouched in one line, `Q-TAG-1 to Q-TAG-12 still queued: <reason>`: only that verdict, only a reason of ten characters or more, the lower key first, both ends of one repository's tag, every key between them and the ends counting.

**What the check cannot see.** Whether the quoted line means what the plan says it means, and whether the reason given for leaving a roadmap item out is a good one. It makes the evidence openable and the omission stated.

**The general form.** A plan's reasons are lines a reader can open and a statement for every item it leaves alone, and the gate asks for both at the moment the plan goes up.
