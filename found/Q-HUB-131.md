Q-HUB-131: a resumed plan is not a new plan. The first version of the cost instrument counted a re-approval as a second plan; the approval carries the plan's title and the script now merges same-title approvals. Two different plans with one title would be merged, and an approval with no title line starts a new plan (found in step 30 of the research plan of 2 November, filed in step 54 of the redesign plan).
