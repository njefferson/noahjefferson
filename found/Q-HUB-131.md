The first version of the cost instrument counted a re-approval as a second plan; the approval carries the plan's title and the script now merges same-title approvals. Two different plans with one title would be merged, and an approval with no title line starts a new plan (found in step 30 of the research plan of 2 November, filed in step 54 of the redesign plan).

Put right already: "The same plan approved again after an amendment is one plan" in `session-cost.mjs`. The cost script merges approvals that carry one title into one plan.
