Q-HUB-537: A review goes stale when a sibling step ends while the review runs. In the photography app's session on 11 November, a review for a resend began at 13:36, and a sibling step that shared no file with it ended at 13:37. The review named that sibling as the step judged, the gate read the review as begun before that end, and the review cleared nothing, so a second review was needed. With six steps running side by side this repeats. The repair suggested was that a review judges the end it was sent for, which is the step its Next line names.
